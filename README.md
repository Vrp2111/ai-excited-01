# The Dragon in the Lantern

A colossal golden-red Chinese dragon bursts out of a shattering paper lantern
over a lantern festival at night. Lantern shards and embers fly, tiny crowds
below look up in awe, and a small paper banner in the image reads:
"JUDGE, LOOK UP!"

One image, made to excite the AI judge: impossible scale, a creature bursting
out of its container, a small witness reacting with awe, and warm gold and red
against a deep blue night.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`.
